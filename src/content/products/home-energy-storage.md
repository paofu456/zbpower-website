## Storage for homes and small commercial systems

ZBPOWER builds wall-mounted and stackable lithium battery systems for residential and small commercial energy storage. The platform is intended for projects that need a stable battery module with a practical installation format.

## System priorities

- Long cycle life for daily storage operation
- High reliability across repeated charge and discharge cycles
- Efficient system cost for residential and small commercial projects
- Enclosure and communication options matched to the wider storage system

## Installation formats

Wall-mounted configurations suit installations with limited floor space. Stackable configurations allow the usable capacity to be planned as part of the overall system architecture.

## OEM and ODM support

Our team can review the target module count, enclosure, BMS, communication and installation requirements before building a first article and moving the configuration into production.

Contact us with the intended application, capacity range and system interface to discuss the next step.
