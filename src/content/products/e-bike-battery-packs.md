## Built for electric mobility

ZBPOWER develops lithium battery packs for electric bicycles, scooters and motorcycles. Each pack is specified around the vehicle's operating voltage, expected range, available enclosure space and connection to the controller.

The platform is intended for OEM and ODM projects that need a production-ready pack rather than a generic off-the-shelf component.

## Design priorities

- High energy density for longer range within a compact vehicle envelope
- Fast-charging support matched to the battery and charger configuration
- Multi-layer electrical, thermal and mechanical protection
- Stable pack assembly and testing for repeat production

## Application fit

Typical projects include commuter e-bikes, cargo and utility bicycles, e-scooters and light electric motorcycles. Final configuration depends on the vehicle's duty cycle, local requirements and target cost.

## From requirement to production

Our team reviews the application, selects the cell and BMS approach, builds a first article, validates the configuration and then moves it into batch production in Dongguan.

Contact us with the target voltage, capacity, enclosure dimensions and expected annual volume to begin a specification review.
